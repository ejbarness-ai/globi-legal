# 🌍 Globi

**Learn geography through fun quizzes.** Explore regions, earn badges, keep a streak, and climb the global leaderboard — with Globi, your globe-shaped study buddy.

Globi is a free geography learning game for iPhone with 4,700+ fact-checked questions across 7 world regions: countries, flags, capitals, physical geography, and more.

## Support

Having trouble, found a wrong answer, or want to suggest a feature? Email us — we read everything:

**ejbarness@icloud.com**

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your iOS version and what you were doing when it happened.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?** No — the whole game works offline without one. An account is only for backing up progress and joining the global leaderboard.

**How do I delete my account and data?** In the app: Settings → Sync Progress → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

**A question seems wrong.** Geography changes! Email us the question text and we'll verify and fix it in the next update.
