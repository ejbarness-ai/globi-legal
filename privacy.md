# Globi Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 4, 2026**

Globi ("the app") is a geography learning game. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have. The short version: **you can play the entire game without an account and without sending us anything.** If you choose to create an account, we store the minimum needed to sync your progress and run the leaderboard — nothing else.

## Playing without an account

All game content works offline and without signing in. Your progress (XP, level, streaks, badges, quiz history, settings) is stored **only on your device**. It never leaves your device unless you create an account, and it is removed when you delete the app or use **Settings → Reset Progress**.

## If you create an account

Creating an account is optional. It exists to back up your progress, sync between devices, and place you on the global leaderboard. When you sign up we collect and store:

- **Email address** — used only to sign you in and recover your account. Never shown to other players, never used for marketing.
- **Display name and avatar (an emoji you pick)** — shown on the public leaderboard alongside your level and XP. Display names are filtered for offensive language. Pick any name; it does not need to be your real one.
- **Gameplay progress** — XP, level, streaks, badges, and quiz statistics, stored so we can restore them on your devices.

If you use **Sign in with Apple** or **Sign in with Google**, we receive your email address (Apple lets you hide it behind a private relay address) and a sign-in token. We receive no other information from Apple or Google.

Account data is stored with our hosting provider, **Supabase** (Postgres database hosted in the United States), which processes it on our behalf and per our instructions.

## What we do NOT do

- No advertising, and no ad networks or SDKs.
- No analytics or behavioral tracking of any kind.
- No selling, renting, or sharing of your information with third parties.
- No push-notification marketing. Reminders (if you enable them) are scheduled locally on your device and never touch a server.
- No collection of location, contacts, photos, microphone, or any device data.

## Deleting your data

- **Server data:** in the app, go to Settings → Sync Progress → **Delete Account**. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, email, display name, and all synced progress from our servers. It cannot be undone.
- **Local data:** use Settings → Reset Progress, or simply delete the app.

## Children

Globi is suitable for all ages. Accounts are intended for users 13 and older (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). Younger players can use the full game without an account, in which case no information is collected. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
